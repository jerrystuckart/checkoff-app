## 6A — Champion / Stakeholder Selection **[NEW structure, RECOVERED inputs]**

Identify from the DAP artifact's `extracted` fields (never re-derived by this specialist —
DAP already produced them): primary champion, backup champions, decision makers, influencers,
DMO/CVB, chamber, city, economic development, other local stakeholders.
`destinationRelationship.ts`'s `DestinationContactContext` (role, sentiment, `isChampion`,
`isBlocker`, `introducedBy`) is the exact shape this selection populates.

## 6B — Relationship Strategy **[NEW]**

For the selected champion: best entry person, channel, framing, likely pain point, why CheckOff
matters to them specifically (not generic partner messaging), timing considerations pulled from
DAP's `timingConsiderations`.

## 6C — First Outreach **[RECOVERED pattern, Willcox]**

Personalized email/DM/contact route — Willcox's own outreach led with a specific in-person
presentation (July 3, Cochise College) rather than a cold email alone; channel choice should be
whatever the relationship strategy (6B) actually supports, not a default template.

## 6D — Follow-Up **[RECOVERED — `destinationRelationship.ts`]**

Chief owns timing/resume via the existing `WAITING` + `nextCheckAt` primitive — no new temporal
concept needed. `FOLLOW_UP` is the real catch-all hub in the relationship-stage graph; most
non-linear jumps (a reply asks for a one-pager, a champion goes quiet, a meeting surfaces a new
stakeholder) route back through it.

## 6E — Progressive Sales Assets **[RECOVERED — `destinationRelationship.ts requiredAssetLevel()`]**

- `LEVEL_0_OUTREACH_MESSAGE` — personalized outreach only (`RELATIONSHIP_READY` through
  `WAITING_FOR_REPLY`).
- `LEVEL_1_ONE_PAGER` — destination-specific one-pager (`ENGAGED`, `MATERIAL_REQUESTED`).
- `LEVEL_2_VISUALS` — destination visuals/mockups/screenshots/examples (meeting stages).
- `LEVEL_3_PITCH_DECK` — full tailored deck, gated on `hasQualifiedDap` — **never generated for
  every cold lead**, only once a stage past meeting is reached AND DAP qualification supports it.

## 6F — Meetings **[NEW structure over RECOVERED primitives]**

Meeting request → scheduling → prep (stakeholder brief assembled from the dossier — DVA/DAP
summary, prior contact history, current ask) → objective → post-meeting notes/actions. Meeting
request and scheduling are `NEEDS_JERRY` (`destination_relationship.create_calendar_event` is
`APPROVAL_REQUIRED`) — Chief prepares everything, never creates the calendar event itself.

## 6G — Proposal / Pricing **[RECOVERED, exact — Willcox 2026-08-08]**

State the champion price once, early. Explain optional Partner initiatives in detail later, not
buried inline with the headline price. **Forbidden in customer-facing copy** (retrieved, exact):
"if the chamber does not renew," "lists disappear," and "already live" before signature.
`proposalViolatesRetrievedRules()` in `destinationHubLifecycle.ts` checks proposal text against
this list mechanically — this is not a style suggestion, it is a hard content check. Every
proposal/pricing/negotiation/commitment step is `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` — Chief drafts, Jerry decides.

## 6H — Destination Content Build **[RECOVERED, exact requirement]**

Content is not limited to commercial businesses: include parks, trails, photo ops, pools,
walkways, landmarks, public places, unusual experiences, wine/food where relevant.
`contentInventoryIsCommercialOnly()` in `destinationHubLifecycle.ts` is a hard check — a content
build that is 100% `businesses` with zero non-commercial content fails this check and should not
proceed to D10.

## 6I — Partner-Friendly Review Artifact **[RECOVERED, exact — the Willcox lesson]**

Never hand a local partner the giant internal research workbook (source URLs, scoring notes,
pricing math, competitor analysis). Produce the pared-down artifact instead: name, include/exclude,
who verified/contacted, comment — nothing else. `buildPartnerFriendlyReview()` in
`destinationHubLifecycle.ts` is the exact transformation; the discipline is entirely in what it
*excludes*, not in any additional processing.

## 6J — Local-Business Activation **[RECOVERED]**

Reuses the existing Business Photo Outreach playbook unchanged: confirmation, photo, Featured Kit,
cling, outreach/follow-up. No new mechanism per destination.

## 6K — Hub Activation **[RECOVERED — always `APPROVAL_REQUIRED`]**

Destination zone, hero, lists, content, routing. `destination_hub.hub_activation` has no AUTO or
AUTO_TELL path — Chief prepares readiness, Jerry activates.

## 6L — Ongoing Relationship **[NEW]**

Support, refresh, reporting, renewal, future opportunities — modeled as the `D17_ONGOING_RELATIONSHIP`
terminal stage plus recurring dossier checks (staleness, budget-window timing) rather than a new
stage machine of its own; see `destinationDossier.ts`'s portfolio ranking.

## NEEDS_JERRY boundary for this methodology (restated from the Phase 2D task spec, not a new rule)

Routine research/qualification stays AUTO. Escalate to Jerry specifically when: a credible
champion wants a call/Zoom/in-person meeting; a meaningful pricing/budget discussion starts; a
proposal is requested; negotiation begins; a commercial commitment is on the table; a reply is
sensitive/high-value/ambiguous; or a stakeholder conflict needs founder judgment. This matches
`standingAuthority.ts`'s existing `destination_relationship.*` and `destination_hub.*`
`APPROVAL_REQUIRED` entries exactly — this methodology does not introduce a parallel escalation
rule, it names when those entries actually fire in practice.
