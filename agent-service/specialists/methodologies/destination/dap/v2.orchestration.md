# DAP v2.0 — Chief Orchestration Wrapper

Chief-facing contract around `v2.md`. Never restates the rubric. The full
DAP artifact remains the authoritative source for a human reading it —
everything below is Chief's OPERATIONAL PROJECTION of it, per the Phase 2G
task's explicit instruction ("The full DAP artifact remains the
authoritative source. Structured extraction is only an operational
projection.").

## Input contract

- Required prior-stage artifact: a `DVA2Artifact` for the SAME
  `destinationId`, with `recommendedNextStep === 'BUILD_DAP_NOW'`
  (`dapEntryConditionMet()`), referenced by `consumedDva2ArtifactRef` on
  the resulting `DAPArtifact` — validated by `validateDapInput()`.

## Expected structured output (`DAPExtractedFields`, `destinationHubLifecycle.ts`)

Extracted from the rubric's own sections, never invented:

- `recommendedChampion`, `secondaryChampions` — Section 12 (Coalition Recommendation) / Section 24
- `decisionMakers`, `stakeholderOrganizations` — Section 11 (Partner Responsibilities)
- `fundingBudgetClues` — Section 2 (Commercial Planning Assumptions) + Section 3 (Timing Assessment budget-cycle signals)
- `destinationPainPoints` — carried forward from DVA-2 Section 7, referenced in DAP's Executive Summary
- `objectionsHurdles` — Section 7 (Acquisition Execution Guidance: Likely Objections)
- `timingConsiderations` — Section 3 (Timing Assessment) + Recommended Start Date
- `checkoffValueProposition` — Section 7 (Best Conversation Starter / What to Lead With)
- `recommendedEntryStrategy` — Section 7 (First Outreach Target + Meeting Objective)
- `relationshipSequence` — Section 4 (Relationship Cadence) dates/actions
- `recommendedOfferDirection` — Section 2 (Standard Champion Price / Founder Price if applicable)
- **`rightNowTask`** (new field, not in the prior placeholder) — Section 21 ("RIGHT NOW"): `{ currentStage, currentGoal, highestPriorityTask, targetDate, estimatedTime, expectedResult, whyItMatters }`. This is the single most operationally important field — it is literally "the next work session," by the rubric's own design ("Only ONE task should appear here").

## Gate fields / escalation behavior

DAP produces a PLAN. Nothing about receiving a DAP artifact itself
triggers `NEEDS_JERRY` — the plan is prepared, then surfaced. Escalation
happens downstream, exactly where it already did before this ingestion:
sending any outreach, creating a calendar event, or making a commercial
commitment remains `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` in `standingAuthority.ts`
regardless of what the plan recommends (see
`v2.legacy-operator-instructions.md`).

## Artifact identity requirement

`DAPArtifact.destinationId` must match both the execution's own
destinationId AND `DVA2Artifact.destinationId` — enforced by
`validateDapInput()` plus the runtime's own `assertExecutionIdentity`.
