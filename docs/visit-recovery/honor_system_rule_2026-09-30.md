# Honor-system rule: time in the configured zone earns a chance to confirm (2026-09-30)

**Rule.** CheckOff is an honor system. Time inside an item's configured zone for the configured dwell earns the user a chance to confirm an experience. It is never an automatic completion, never awards points, and never requires proof of the precise activity. Being in the piazza near the Duomo is enough to offer the Duomo suggestion. Opt-in, confirmation, dismissal, privacy and duplicate protection are unchanged.

## 2. `manual_only` review
"The activity cannot be proven from a location" is **no longer a reason to exclude**. All 144 active `manual_only` items were reviewed (`20260930h`, source `curated_2026-09-30b`). **54 now use the profile that fits their fixed place** (16 outdoor, 15 event, 12 landmark, 7 attraction, 3 retail, 1 restaurant); **90 remain excluded** because there is no meaningful fixed zone.

Flipped (examples): Ristorante Da Adolfo → restaurant (a stay at the restaurant prompts the boat-to-lunch experience); Grotta dello Smeraldo → attraction; Amalfi Coast Sea Kayak, parasailing, Lucibello sail → event at the operator's base; Nocelle, Montepertuso, Fiordo di Furore → outdoor; Chiesa di San Giacomo → attraction (shares a coordinate with Santa Maria Assunta: both are offered, the user confirms what they did); Denver Union Station clock, Munich Neues Rathaus, SD USS Recruit/topiary/Cardiff Kook, Tucson El Tiradito/Rattlesnake Bridge → landmark; Skydive Buckeye, Lake Pleasant Cruises, Desert Belle, Daisy Mountain Railroad, Salt River Tubing, Tucson stables/horseback operators, Point Loma Sportfishing → event at the base; Tucson Mount Lemmon Ski Valley, Vienna Spider Rock and Watersports Arena → attraction.

Kept excluded on purpose: routes/loops/trails/greenways/scenic drives (Sentiero degli Dei, Sentiero dei Limoni, Valle delle Ferriere, Denver loops and hikes, Tempe Town Lake loop, Camelback…), road trips and city-level tasks (Door County, Chicago, Las Vegas, "Go shopping in Kenosha"), multi-stop crawls, dated one-off events and multi-block street fairs (Oktoberfest, Tucson Meet Yourself, Fourth Avenue Street Fair, Mistfest…), brief window/drive-thru stops, tasks with no named place, events with no coordinates (Oide Wiesn, Teufelsrad, Tollwood), and Positano Home Cooking (its coordinate is the company address, not where it happens). The four windows/drive-thrus are excluded for duration, not for provability.

## 3. Past evidence under the clarified rule (provenance kept)
`visit_reevaluate_sessions` re-judges closed sessions discarded only as `below_ignore_band` whose stored entry fix satisfies the zone rule (distance and accuracy), using the observed enter and exit times. It created **5 suggestions** for Jerry (Chiostro del Paradiso 38.8 min, La Tramontina 36.1, Pasticceria Pansa 32.2, Arsenale 31.9, La Scuderia del Duca 32.5), each flagged `metadata.reevaluated = true` with `rule`, `originalOutcome`, `reevaluatedAt` and `sessionId`; the session row is marked `candidate_reevaluated`. Newest stay per place wins, already-checked-off and duplicate-pending guards applied, nothing backdated, nothing confirmed. The inverse was also applied: a pending suggestion whose entry fix **fails** the zone rule (the 202-minute Da Adolfo candidate from the lodging session) was withdrawn (`20260930k`), keeping its session row as provenance.

## 4. Review of the evidence changes
**How rejected-entry retries run in the background.** There is no timer. When the server rejects an enter as `fix_uncertain` / `fix_outside_venue` / `stale_fix`, the phone stores a pending enter (no coordinates). It is retried, with a brand-new fix, at the next **real wake-up**: any geofence callback, a sentinel or drift refresh (they now flush the queue after refreshing), a native movement wake-up (new binary), or the app coming to the foreground. Retries stop after 90 minutes. A suspended app gets no CPU from a JS timer, so a retry happens at the next wake-up or not at all, and iOS will not re-fire *enter* for a region it believes you are already in.

**`last_inside_at` and estimated dwell (corrected 2026-09-30).** `last_inside_at` is only "last time a trustworthy fix placed the phone inside". It is not used as presence on its own. When a session is closed **without** an observed exit, the server credits `sampled_inside_s` = the sum over consecutive inside-samples of **min(gap, 15 min)**. **This is an estimate, not a proven minimum**: two samples 15 minutes apart do not show the phone stayed in the zone between them, and the 15-minute cap only limits how far one sample can stretch. It is precise enough to offer an honor-system suggestion (the user decides), so it may support a suggestion, but it is always stored and shown as an estimate: `metadata.dwellBasis = 'estimated'` (+ `sampledSeconds`), the session outcome is `missed_exit_estimated`, the card reads *"about N min, estimated from a few location checks (we didn't see you leave)"*, it is medium confidence at most and never a push. Three kinds of dwell are kept distinguishable: **observed** (enter and exit events; no `dwellBasis`), **estimated** (`dwellBasis = 'estimated'`), and **re-evaluated** (`reevaluated = true`, a past observed stay judged under a newer rule). The words "at least" are reserved for evidence that establishes a minimum; nothing in the system currently does, so the UI never says it. (Migration `20260930m` renamed `proven_inside_s` → `sampled_inside_s`, `dwell_lower_bound_min` → `dwell_estimate_min`, relabelled existing rows; old `dwellBound='lower'` is still read as estimated.) Check: `visit_zone_dwell_rule.sql`, `visit_presence_evidence.sql`.
