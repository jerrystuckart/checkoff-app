## No substantive Jerry-approval gate found for STARTING DAP itself

Unlike DVA-1 and DVA-2, this document has no "Final Statement" claiming DAP itself requires approval to begin — its entry condition is simply a completed, GREEN-path DVA-2 (per DVA-2's own Recommended Next Step = "Build DAP now"). The document is explicit, repeatedly, that DAP's OUTPUT still requires Jerry for anything commercial or outbound:

- "If Jerry changes pricing after reviewing DVA-2, use Jerry's updated business decision instead."
- "Do not recalculate Champion or Destination Partner pricing."
- Section 4 (Relationship Cadence) and Section 7 (Acquisition Execution Guidance) produce a **plan**, not an executed action — sending anything, scheduling anything, or committing to anything remains `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` in `standingAuthority.ts` regardless of what DAP recommends. This is not a legacy-operator-instruction question; it is the same real, substantive outbound-authority boundary Phase 2C/2D/2E already enforced, and this ingestion changes nothing about it.

already on file from the Willcox Open Brain thoughts.
