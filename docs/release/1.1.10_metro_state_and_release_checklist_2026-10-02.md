# 1.1.10 (build 155): metro state fix and release checklist, 2026-10-02

OTA 01a0fe7d-24b3-7fbd-8d87-59723749265f (group 2be06aa2-a878-4917-88ef-7faea4f66b03), branch/channel production, runtime
86ac0036db685dec7e1921f661d6b77ede23965e, iOS only, commit 951a137. 1.1.9 runtime (81dbd1f1) was not touched.

## Root cause
- lib/explicitMetroIntent.js held the link intent in a module variable for the life of the JS process; iOS keeps that process across a flight.
- screens/HomeScreen.jsx foreground effect returned early when the phone was outside every launched metro (`if (!nearest) return`), and
  shouldAdoptNearestMetro refused to adopt while an intent existed, so Amalfi was never revisited.
- Coverage (UNSUPPORTED) is derived from live GPS, the header from selectedMetro: two sources, hence "Amalfi Coast" plus "We haven't unlocked this city yet".
- preserved-short-interruption was consistent with its location (a session recomputed at Heathrow was saved under the stale Amalfi id); the cache is not why Amalfi stayed.
- "LOCAL" next to the streak is the points tier (lib/tiers.js), not geography.

## Contract
exact item/list link > metro link > manual Switch City (newest wins) > persisted choice > physical metro. Intent is temporary: dropped on resume after
60 min, or by a fix more than 50 km from where it was set. A changed physical metro is adopted without relaunch. Outside every launched metro: header
keeps the browsed metro with a BROWSING badge and "CheckOff has not launched near your current location"; with nothing browsed the picker says the same.

## Cache rules
Short-interruption preserve: age <= 5 min AND moved <= 2 km (was 160 km). Fingerprint-match path still bounded by MAX_NEARBY_RADIUS_M.

## Seasonal lists
Positano Essentials and Positano Beyond the Postcard had ends_at 2027-12-31 (placeholder from 20260927_amalfi_04_positano_hub.sql). Set to NULL (migration 20261002_*).
Countdown is also suppressed for ends more than 366 days out (lib/seasonCountdown.js). No other active list is affected.
