# CheckOff Editorial Methodology — v1

`methodologyId: checkoff_editor` `version: v1` `owningSpecialist: checkoff_editor`

**Status: [NEW]** — this methodology is not recovered from a prior written document. No source in
the repo or Open Brain names a formal "CheckOff editorial style guide." What exists is scattered
real output (partner-messaging copy in Open Brain, live item text in the database) that this
document distills into an explicit, checkable set of rules for the first time. Treat this as the
Phase 2D baseline, subject to revision once Jerry reviews real editorial output against it.

## What this specialist owns

The transformation from a **verified factual experience** (output of `research_verifier` after
M6/`D` verification) to a **final CheckOff item** — never the other direction. `checkoff_editor`
runs strictly after verification, never blended into M3 discovery or M6 verification notes.

## Required principles

1. **Compelling hook in the first 5–7 words.** The reader decides whether to keep reading in that
   span — lead with the specific, interesting thing, not a category label.
2. **Action/experience first, business naturally incorporated.** The item describes what you *do*;
   the venue is where you do it, introduced in service of the action, not as the subject of the
   sentence.
3. **Specific, not generic.** Name the actual thing — the dish, the exhibit, the trail, the
   exact experience — never a vague category ("great tacos" is filler; "the birria ramen at
   [specific place]" is a CheckOff item).
4. **Sounds like a local recommendation, not a listing.** Write the way a knowledgeable local
   friend would describe it to someone new in town, not the way a directory or brochure would.
5. **Concise.** Every word earns its place. Cut qualifiers and hedges.
6. **Energetic.** Convey why this is worth doing, not just that it exists.
7. **No generic tourism-directory language.** Avoid stock phrases ("a must-see," "hidden gem" used
   as a crutch rather than a real hidden-gem experience, "something for everyone").
8. **Never change the underlying researched fact.** If verification says the venue, hours,
   price, or specific item is X, the CheckOff wording still says X — editorial license covers
   phrasing and framing, never the fact.
9. **Never invent a menu item, product, or experience not present in the source research.** If the
   research doesn't name a specific dish/exhibit/activity, `checkoff_editor` does not fabricate
   one to sound more specific — it either uses what's there or flags the item back to
   `research_verifier` for a more specific pass.

## Avoid generic openings

Do not start items with `Visit`, `Explore`, `Check out`, or `Stop by` unless the specific sentence
genuinely calls for it (rare — these openings are almost always a sign the hook isn't specific
enough yet). Prefer starting mid-action or with the specific thing itself.

## Required output shape

Every `checkoff_editor` result envelope must carry **both**, so Chief can validate editorial
fidelity without re-deriving it:

- `factualSource` — the exact researched statement this item is based on (verbatim from
  `research_verifier`'s M6/verification output).
- `checkoffizedItem` — the final wording.

Chief (via `validateResultEnvelope`) treats a result missing either field as incomplete evidence —
"final wording produced" alone is never accepted as proof the fact was preserved.

## Example transformation shape (illustrative, not a real CheckOff item)

- `factualSource`: "Sonoran Glass School offers a make-your-own-glass class, walk-ins welcome
  Tuesday–Saturday."
- `checkoffizedItem`: "Blow your own glass ornament at Sonoran Glass School — no experience
  needed, just show up Tuesday through Saturday."

Note the hook ("Blow your own glass ornament") lands in the first few words, the business is
introduced in service of the action, and every fact (walk-in, days) survives unchanged.

## Escalation

`checkoff_editor` never independently changes strategic scope or invents facts to fill a gap. If
the source research is too generic to editorialize honestly (principle 9), it returns the item to
`research_verifier` with `blockers: ['insufficient specificity for editorial pass']` rather than
producing filler.
