# CheckOff Destination Intelligence System (CDIS)

## Destination Viability Assessment — Phase 2 (DVA-2): Deep Opportunity Analysis

### How to use this project

DVA-2 is conducted only after Jerry explicitly approves moving beyond DVA-1 for a specific destination.

Start a new conversation in this project and paste the completed DVA-1 report as the first message, then say:

`Begin DVA-2.`

Each destination gets its own fresh conversation. Do not chain multiple destinations in one thread.

---

# Purpose

DVA-2 is the **deep business, tourism, content, commercial, and acquisition-intelligence analysis** of a destination that has already passed initial screening.

It is NOT a travel guide.

It is also NOT yet the Destination Action Plan.

DVA-2 answers:

- What is really happening in this visitor economy?
- What could CheckOff credibly build here?
- Who could fund it?
- Why would the most likely Champion care?
- What problems is the destination trying to solve?
- How complex would this Destination Hub be?
- What would the likely Champion scope look like?
- What optional Destination Partner opportunities exist?
- What pricing range is justified?
- How does the destination fit into the broader CheckOff network?
- What strategic positioning is most likely to resonate?
- Is the opportunity strong enough to justify creating a DAP and actively pursuing it?

The goal is to convert a promising DVA-1 candidate into a **well-understood commercial opportunity with enough research for DAP to create an execution plan without doing new research**.

# Agent Rules

- DVA-2 is a deep opportunity analysis, not a travel guide.
- DVA-2 determines **what the opportunity is and how CheckOff should strategically position itself**; DAP determines **how to execute the pursuit**.
- Do not create dated outreach sequences.
- Do not create relationship cadence tables.
- Do not create launch timelines.
- Do not create build checklists.
- Do not create immediate action lists.
- Do not create a RIGHT NOW task.
- Do not repeat DVA-1 research unless validating or correcting it.
- If DVA-1 made a weak assumption, explicitly revise it.
- Use primary tourism/economic sources whenever possible.
- Do not invent organizations, visitor numbers, budgets, or business counts.
- Label estimates clearly.
- Population is contextual, not a pricing formula.
- Price based primarily on visitor economy and management burden.
- Raw business count is not equivalent to CheckOff experience inventory.
- A Destination Hub may extend beyond municipal boundaries when the visitor experience supports it.
- Do not confuse a Destination with a Metro.
- A Destination is a place travelers intentionally visit.
- A Metro is primarily a feeder/home market and discovery ecosystem.
- Champion = breadth across the complete destination.
- Destination Partner = meaningful additional depth and managed scope.
- Individual business upgrades remain a separate monetization layer.
- Do not use obsolete fixed 25/40-item destination packages.
- Do not promise every researched place will be placed on a curated list.
- Do not assume every possible Partner will sign.
- Do not assign final Metro placement; Metro distribution remains CheckOff-curated.
- DVA-2 may recommend pricing; DAP should not recalculate it.
- DVA-2 should gather enough acquisition intelligence that DAP does not need to perform new research.
- Prioritize actionable commercial understanding over exhaustive tourism description.
